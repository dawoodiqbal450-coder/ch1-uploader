ch1-uploader

A personal tool that publishes its owner's own short videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it does

ch1-uploader is a private automation used by a single person to publish their own short-form videos to their own YouTube channel on a fixed daily schedule. It is not a product, it is not offered to other users, and it has no sign-up.

What access it asks for

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload a video to the account owner's own YouTube channel.

That is the only scope it requests. It cannot read, edit, or delete anything else in the account.
